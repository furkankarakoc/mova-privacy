# Privacy Policy — MOVA

**Effective date:** September 12, 2026
**App:** MOVA
**Bundle ID:** `com.afkmobileengine.mova`

MOVA is an offline mini-game collection developed for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information MOVA stores, how it is used, and how purchases are handled.

## 1. Information We Collect

MOVA does not require an account or login and does not operate a developer backend.

MOVA does not collect or transmit personal information through its own systems.

MOVA does not collect:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Contacts
* Precise or approximate location
* Photos or videos
* Camera data
* Microphone or audio data
* Health or fitness data
* Advertising identifiers
* Account credentials

MOVA version 1 does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device

MOVA stores certain information locally on your device to provide and maintain app functionality. This may include:

* Game scores and statistics
* App settings, including sound, haptics, and appearance preferences
* Tutorial and onboarding status
* Daily Challenge history
* Achievement unlocks
* A local cache of MOVA+ purchase entitlement information

This information is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to a MOVA-operated server.

Deleting the MOVA app may remove locally stored app data. Data included in iOS backups may remain subject to the backup settings and policies controlled by the user and Apple.

## 3. Purchases and Subscriptions

MOVA offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

Payments are processed by Apple. MOVA does not receive or store your payment card number, Apple ID password, or other payment credentials.

MOVA may request purchase and entitlement information from Apple to determine whether a MOVA+ purchase or subscription is active and to restore purchases.

Available MOVA+ products may include:

* Monthly subscription
* Yearly subscription
* Lifetime purchase

For information about how Apple processes purchase-related information, please refer to Apple's privacy documentation.

## 4. Advertising and Analytics

MOVA version 1 does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

MOVA does not send advertising or analytics data to third-party advertising or analytics providers.

If third-party advertising or analytics services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 5. Tracking

MOVA does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

MOVA does not use Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for tracking.

## 6. Children's Privacy

MOVA is a general-audience game and is not specifically designed or marketed as a children's app.

MOVA does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Data Security

Because MOVA does not operate a backend that collects personal information, MOVA does not maintain a centralized database of user personal information.

Information stored locally by the app is protected by the security mechanisms provided by iOS and the user's device.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

MOVA does not maintain personal information on its own servers.

Locally stored game data and settings remain on the device until they are deleted by the user, removed by the operating system, or otherwise cleared through normal device or app management.

Uninstalling MOVA generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to iOS backup behavior and settings controlled by the user.

Because MOVA does not operate a server containing user personal information, there is no separate MOVA account or server-side personal-data deletion process.

## 9. Third-Party Services

MOVA relies on Apple's App Store and StoreKit for in-app purchases and purchase entitlement verification.

Apple may process information independently in connection with App Store, Apple ID, and payment services. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's own policies.

MOVA version 1 does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the effective date at the top of this policy will be updated. The current version will be available at the publicly hosted Privacy Policy URL associated with MOVA.
