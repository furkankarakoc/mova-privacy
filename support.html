MOVA+

MOVA+ is an optional upgrade that provides additional board styles, advanced statistics, exclusive effects, and future premium content.

If you purchased MOVA+ and your entitlement is not appearing, open MOVA and go to:

Profile → Settings → Restore Purchases

You can also use the Restore control on the MOVA+ paywall.
